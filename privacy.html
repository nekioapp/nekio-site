NEKIO is an early-stage project developed by DTA Consulting Kft. The current purpose of this website is to validate the concept, to organise research conversations and to get in touch with people who are interested.

2. Scope of this notice and purposes of processing

This notice covers the candidate and employer forms submitted on the NEKIO website, the related communication, and the organisation and documentation of research conversations.

We process data to: receive and answer enquiries; arrange and run research conversations; learn about job-search and hiring experiences; validate the NEKIO concept; contact people interested in a later, voluntary pilot; keep the website secure; and measure website use in aggregate.

We do not use personal data to send marketing messages. The current NEKIO website does not evaluate or rank candidates automatically.

3. The data we process

3.1. Candidate form

Data you can provide: first name, current or most recent role, the role you are looking for, optionally your location and preferred way of working, a description of the difficulties you have met in your job search, and whether you would take part in a research conversation of about 20 minutes. An e-mail address is requested only if you answer yes to the research conversation question; we use it to arrange the conversation. If you answer no, we do not ask for or process an e-mail address.

3.2. Employer form

Data you can provide: first name, work e-mail address, company name, job title, information about the positions you are hiring for or find hard to fill, optionally the recruiting system you use, a description of difficulties in finding candidates, and whether you are interested in a later pilot.

Please do not include special categories of personal data (for example information about health, ethnic origin, religion or political opinion) or data about other people in the free-text fields of the forms.

3.3. Notes and audio recordings of research conversations

After prior information and separate, documented consent, a research conversation may be audio-recorded. Recording is voluntary; you can take part without a recording where that is compatible with running the research. Research notes may also be taken.

We use a recording only to document the conversation and to write the research notes. We do not use recordings for marketing or public release.

3.4. Technical data

When you access the website, the hosting provider may process technical data needed for operation and security, such as your IP address, the time of access and technical log data. To measure website use in aggregate, NEKIO uses the GoatCounter web analytics service, which works without cookies. The service compiles statistics from technical browser data (for example the page opened, the referring page, the browser type and the screen size) and from key actions on the website (for example opening or submitting a form). The service does not receive the data you enter in the forms.

The website does not use cookies or advertising scripts. Your language choice (EN/HU) is saved in your browser’s local storage so the site remembers it; it stays on your device.

3.5. Consent record and campaign data

With each submission we store the language the site was shown in, a record of your consent (the time and the version of this notice) and — if you arrived through a link carrying campaign tags (utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign) — those tags, so we can see which post or page brought you here. We keep the consent record so that we can demonstrate that consent was given.

4. Legal basis

We process the personal data you give in the forms on the NEKIO website on the basis of your consent, in accordance with Article 6(1)(a) GDPR, for the purposes stated on the form: the research, the aggregated analysis of answers, and contact about the research and a possible later pilot.

Consent is a freely given, specific, informed and unambiguous indication of your wishes. You can withdraw it at any time by sending a message to dataprotection@dta-consulting.hu. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before the withdrawal.

If you withdraw your consent, the controller stops processing the data that is based on it and deletes the data, unless a legal requirement or another suitable legal basis justifies keeping it.

Audio-recording a research conversation is a separate processing operation. A recording is made only after the participant has been informed in advance and has given separate consent. We record that consent in a documentable way (in writing, or orally at the start of the recording) before the conversation begins.

The legal basis for the technical data that arises when you access the website (section 3.4) is the controller’s legitimate interest in running the website securely and reliably and in measuring and improving website use in aggregate, under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

5. Storage and service providers

5.1. GitHub Pages

The NEKIO website is served through GitHub Pages. In connection with serving the site and operating it securely, the provider may process technical data. Use of the service is governed by GitHub’s privacy statement and contractual terms in force from time to time.

5.2. Supabase

NEKIO stores the data submitted through the website forms in the Supabase database service. The database runs in a region within the European Union. Only persons authorised by the controller can access the data.

The controller applies access controls and sets up the database permissions so that website visitors cannot access other people’s application data. Supabase may process the data on the controller’s behalf to the extent necessary to provide the service.

The providers’ privacy statements and data-processing terms govern the use of their services. As regards any data transfers, the controller acts according to the providers’ actual contractual terms.

5.3. GoatCounter

Website use is measured by the GoatCounter service. The provider may process technical data for this purpose. Use of the service is governed by GoatCounter’s privacy statement and terms in force from time to time.

6. Transfers and access

Personal data can be accessed by those authorised persons of the controller who need it to handle enquiries, run research conversations or operate the website. Technical providers may process data to the extent necessary to provide their services.

The controller does not sell personal data and, in the current validation phase, does not pass data submitted through the forms to employers in order to present candidates. If a transfer is planned during a later pilot, we will inform data subjects beforehand in an appropriate way and act only on a suitable legal basis.

7. Retention

As a rule, we keep application data and related communication for up to 12 months from the last substantive contact — or, if no contact took place, from the submission — and then delete it or, where that is enough for the purpose, irreversibly anonymise it, unless a legal requirement or another suitable legal basis justifies keeping it longer.

Audio recordings of research conversations are kept for up to 90 days from the completion of the research notes, and then deleted. Only authorised persons can access the recordings. Participants are told the purpose, who has access and the deletion time before recording.

The retention period of technical log data depends on how the relevant provider operates and on the time needed for security purposes.

8. Data security

The controller applies appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data. Their aim is to prevent unauthorised access to, alteration, disclosure, loss or destruction of personal data.

Access to the database is limited to authorised persons. The controller sets up the system permissions so that website visitors cannot access other people’s application data, and ensures that access rights are properly managed and that services are configured securely.

9. Your rights

Under the conditions set out in the GDPR, you may have the right to request access to your personal data, to request its rectification or erasure, to request restriction of processing and, where the legal conditions are met, to data portability.

Because processing of form data is based on consent, you can withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before the withdrawal.

Requests can be sent to dataprotection@dta-consulting.hu. The controller handles requests within the time limit set by the GDPR; as a rule it replies within one month of receipt.
