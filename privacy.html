2. What we collect

Candidate form: name, e-mail address, current or most recent role, the kind of role you are looking for, your location and preferred working arrangement (optional), the biggest frustration in your job search, and whether you are willing to take part in a 20-minute research conversation.

Employer form: name, work e-mail address, company, job title, the roles you are hiring for or struggling to fill, the ATS or hiring system you use (optional), your biggest challenge in finding suitable candidates, and whether you would consider an early, manually operated pilot.

With each submission we also store the language the site was shown in, a record of your consent (the time and the version of this notice), and — if you arrived through a link carrying campaign tags (utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign) — those tags, so we can see which post or page brought you here.

We do not ask for CVs, identity documents or sensitive personal information. Please do not include sensitive details (for example about health, ethnicity, religion or political views) or information about other people in the free-text answers.

  • To learn from the answers (in aggregated form) so we can decide whether and how to build NEKIO.

Our legal basis is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you give by ticking the box on the form.
