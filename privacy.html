NEKIO is an early-stage project developed by DTA Consulting Kft. The current purpose of this website is to validate the concept, organise research conversations and get in touch with people who are interested.

2. Scope of this notice and purposes of processing

This notice covers the candidate and employer forms submitted on the NEKIO website, the related communication, and the organisation and documentation of research conversations.

We process data to: receive and answer enquiries; arrange and run research conversations; learn about job-search and hiring experiences; validate the NEKIO concept; contact people interested in a later, voluntary pilot; and keep the website secure.

The current NEKIO website does not evaluate or rank candidates automatically. We do not use personal data to send marketing messages.

3. The data we process

3.1. Candidate form

Data you can provide: name, e-mail address, current or most recent role, the role you are looking for, optionally your location and preferred way of working, a description of the difficulties you have met in your job search, and whether you would take part in a research conversation of about 20 minutes.

3.2. Employer form

Data you can provide: name, work e-mail address, company name, job title, information about the positions you are hiring for or find hard to fill, optionally the recruiting system you use, a description of difficulties in finding candidates, and whether you are interested in a later pilot.

3.3. Notes and audio recordings of research conversations

After prior information and separate, documented consent, a research conversation may be audio-recorded. Recording is voluntary; you can take part without a recording where that is compatible with running the research. Research notes may also be taken.

We use a recording only to document the conversation and to write the research notes. We do not use it for marketing or public release.

3.4. Technical data

When you access the website, the hosting provider may process technical data needed for operation and security, such as your IP address, the time of access and technical log data. NEKIO currently does not use a separate web analytics service.

4. Legal basis

For answering enquiries and arranging research conversations, the legal basis may, depending on the circumstances, be the controller’s legitimate interest under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Before relying on it, the controller must document the balancing of its legitimate interest against the rights of data subjects.

The legal basis for making and using audio recordings is the data subject’s prior, separate and documented consent. Refusing consent must not lead to any disadvantage. Before the recording, the data subject is informed of the purpose, who has access and how long it is kept.

The legal basis and retention rules for technical data are to be determined based on how the providers actually operate and the website’s security needs.

5. Storage and service providers

5.1. GitHub Pages

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The service makes the website available on the internet and, in connection with its operation, may process technical data. The provider’s current privacy and contractual terms, and the details of technical logging, must be checked before launch.

5.2. Supabase

Data submitted through the website forms is stored in a Supabase database in an EU region. Only persons authorised by the controller can access the database. The access controls and Row Level Security settings must be checked and tested before launch.

The providers’ roles as processors, their contractual terms, sub-processors and any transfers to third countries are to be finalised based on the actual configuration and the providers’ documentation. Using an EU region does not in itself rule out other international data-handling or access situations.

6. Transfers and access

Personal data can be accessed by those authorised persons of the controller who need it to handle enquiries, run research conversations or operate the website. Technical providers may process data to the extent necessary to provide their services.

The controller does not sell personal data and, in the current validation phase, does not pass data submitted through the forms to employers in order to present candidates. If a transfer is planned during a later pilot, we will inform data subjects beforehand in an appropriate way and act only on a suitable legal basis.

7. Retention

As a rule, we keep application data and related communication for up to 12 months from the last substantive contact, and then delete it or — where that is enough for the purpose — irreversibly anonymise it, unless a legal requirement or another suitable legal basis justifies keeping it longer.

Audio recordings of research conversations are kept for up to 90 days from the completion of the research notes, and then deleted. Only authorised persons can access the recordings. Participants are told the purpose, who has access and the deletion time before recording.

The retention period of technical log data is to be determined based on the providers’ actual settings and the security purposes required.

8. Data security

The controller applies appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including limiting access, setting up permissions appropriately, handling secret keys securely, applying necessary updates and preventing unauthorised access.

The Supabase database must be configured so that website visitors cannot access other people’s application data. The actual security settings must be checked and tested before launch.

9. Your rights

Under the conditions set out in the GDPR, you may have the right to request access to your personal data, to request its rectification or erasure, to request restriction of processing, to object to processing based on legitimate interest and, where the legal conditions are met, to data portability.

Where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before the withdrawal.

Requests can be sent to dataprotection@dta-consulting.hu. The controller handles requests within the time limit set by the GDPR; as a rule it replies within one month of receipt.

10. Complaints

You can lodge a complaint with the Hungarian National Authority for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (NAIH).

  • Website: https://www.naih.hu/
  • E-mail: ugyfelszolgalat@naih.hu
  • Postal address: 1363 Budapest, Pf. 9., Hungary

Where the legal conditions are met, you can also go to court.

11. Automated decision-making and profiling

The current version of the NEKIO website does not carry out automated decision-making or profiling based on the data given in the forms. Filling in a form does not lead to automatic candidate evaluation, ranking or a job offer.

12. Changes to this notice

The controller may change this notice if the purposes or methods of processing, the technical solutions or the applicable law change. The version in force is always available on the NEKIO website.
